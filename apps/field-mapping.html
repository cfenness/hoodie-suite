Field Mapping

Map a source dataset's fields to the master schema. A dataset is usually a mix — in All fields you route each field to whatever master it belongs to (identify AB_Brands → brand, Address → outlet.address, Name → outlet.outlet_name) in one pass. Mappings persist; apply pre/post dictionaries on either side of the map.

Pre / Post are data-dictionary transforms — none · trim · upper · lower · title_case · digits_only · size_to_ml · year_from_date. The dictionary picker attaches a saved value dictionary (built in the Dictionary tab, e.g. cab sauv → Cabernet Sauvignon) to a row — it overrides pre/post and applies on the next Apply. Precedence: raw ƒx expression > dictionary > pre/post. Unmapped rows (Master = —) are skipped on save.
