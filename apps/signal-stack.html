Pick a category once — it drives all four instruments below. The path runs Menu → Basket → Store → Price: the earliest signal (what's being poured) to the slowest (shelf price). When they agree, it's a confirmed shift.

Why the four line up: every instrument resolves to the same Canon (MDM) SKU identity — COLA/TTB-anchored, not fragile scraped listing IDs. That shared spine is what lets a menu serve, a basket rank, a store's shelf, and a shelf price all be read as the same product. Replicating the connective tissue is the moat, not the scraping.
