What a total-analysis dossier can actually show — grounded, honest about the gaps

Signal Stack centers a subject, and every instrument reads it off one identity spine. Products resolve to the Canon SKU identity; accounts, chains and geos resolve to outlet identity; census/ACS is the enrichment ring around the place side. This map is the design input we said we were missing: for each subject type, which instruments have real landed data vs. what's coverage-thin, derived, or a gap — so we can see where a real panel is warranted before building the mock.

The read

Product is the mature subject — a real menu→shelf→distribution→price funnel over canon identity. That's what Signal Stack shows today; it's just retail-only.

Account is buildable now — but for a specific reason. Its identity, commercial (territory · relations · locator · banner), and census rings are all real, so an account dossier answers who / where / who-they-are from landed data on day one. What's thin is the outcome signal — what the account actually sells — which is coverage-bound and fills in as the DoorDash / Toast / first-party pulls widen. Census is precisely what makes an account subject valuable before that outcome data arrives.

Zip / geo and demographic segment are census-led: strong as an enrichment lens and a way into the account layer, not standalone dossiers yet. Chain / chain-parent has real footprint + reachability + banner identity, with aggregate assortment filling in chain-by-chain.

The keystone we don't have yet is sales. Depletions / POS turns inferred velocity into real velocity and — the piece worth calling out — lights the brand ↔ demographics bridge: sales-weighted outlets crossed with their trade-area census reveal which demographics over-index on a brand, and the inverse (which brands over-index in a segment). That's sales-gated in the grid, not a gap — the method is ready, it's waiting on the data.

So the v1 mock should center the ACCOUNT subject — light its real rings (identity, commercial, census) fully, honestly stub the thin ones (assortment/velocity), and let the census enrichment carry the value. That proves the polymorphic subject on the data we actually have.

Grounded design map · not a wired app. Every source named is a real table, app, or connector in the suite; every gap is honest. Readiness reflects landed-data maturity, not ambition.
